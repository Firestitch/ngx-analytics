Providers

Built-in providers (googleTagManager, klaviyo) ship with the library and send to those real services. Custom (custom-demo) is the escape hatch — a third-party tracker plugged in through config only. It loads assets/mock-tracker.js and forwards events to the window.mockTracker global it installs. This is the pattern a real marketing pixel (or Cloudcure's own tracker) uses.
